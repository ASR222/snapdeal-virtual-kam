About this data

Gupta Home & Kitchen uses the five metrics from the assignment brief. Everything else is a labelled assumption, added so the KAM can investigate the way a real account manager would:

  • “Orders” are delivered orders; sales = orders × average selling price; cancellation rate = cancelled ÷ placed orders.
  • Listing impressions, visits and conversion are invented for all three sellers.
  • Sharma Ethnic Wear and Raza Mobile Accessories are invented sellers with different root causes (competitor pricing, and lost visibility), to show the KAM diagnoses from data rather than following a script.
  • Product lists, competitor prices, cancellation reasons, ads and hidden-listing details are invented.
  • Rupee impact estimates use simple stated rules, for example a lost product’s orders are estimated from what it sold last month.
  • Follow-up calls are simulated one week after the previous call, with results based on what was agreed.

Every action is simulated. Nothing connects to Snapdeal systems. Call history is stored only in this browser.
